# stackedwidget

Shows one of its children at a time, selected by index.

## At a glance

- **Kind:** `stackedwidget`
- **Category:** Layout
- **Children:** accepted, stacked by the container
- **Used in the shipped interface:** 0 times (available to mods, not used by the base game)

## When to use it

Shows one child at a time.

## Notes

Used by the engine's tool screens; available to mods. The visible child is selected by index, either from a data model or from script.

All widgets also accept the [common properties](../common-properties.md); they are not repeated here.
